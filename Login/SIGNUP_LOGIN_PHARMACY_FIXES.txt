## 📋 COMPLETE WORKFLOW

### For New Users:
1. Go to landing page
2. Click "Sign Up" in navigation
3. Fill in your details:
   - Role: Select "Pharmacist" (or other role)
   - Full Name: Your name
   - Email: Your email
   - Phone: Your phone number (min 10 digits)
   - Password: At least 6 characters
4. Click "Sign Up" button
5. Account created! You can now sign in

### For Returning Users:
1. Click login link on signup page using "← Back to Login"
2. Or go directly to login.html
3. Enter email and password
4. Click "Sign In"
5. Directed to your portal (pharmacist/admin/doctor/patient)

### For Pharmacist - Adding Medicine:
1. After login, Pharmacist Portal opens
2. Click "Add Medicine" button (blue, top-right area)
3. Modal dialog appears
4. Enter medicine details:
   - **Medicine Name**: Must be filled (e.g., "Aspirin", "Paracetamol")
   - **SKU**: Optional but recommended (e.g., "ASP-001")
   - **Manufacturer**: Optional (e.g., "ABC Corp")
   - **Initial Quantity**: Must be a valid number ≥ 0 (e.g., 100)
   - **Unit Price**: Must be a valid number ≥ 0 (e.g., 50.00)
5. Click "Add Medicine" button in modal
6. Success message appears
7. Medicine list refreshes with new medicine added
8. Medicine shows in your inventory with status badge
